# DNA: the T-rex of 31151 "T. rex" (Creator 3-in-1, 2024)

The app's Expert T-rex. Higher tiers mean more detail, not more bulk, so the reference is LEGO's own build, read step by step.

Sources:
- the official instructions: the T-rex is booklet **6520924** (128 p., steps 1–243). 6520925 is the triceratops, 6520926 the pterodactyl. Steps 237–243 are the nest accessory.
- the inventory: `data/golden/31151-inventory.json`, 626 parts + 17 spares.
- the photo: `booklets/rex31151/31151-1.jpg`.
- rendered pages: /tmp/rex31151pages/p-NNN.jpg (page = PDF page; step 1 is on p. 5).

The rebuild is `scripts/rebuild-31151.mjs` → `data/golden/31151-rex.ldr`.

Grid: stud 20 LDU, plate 8, brick 24. Frame: −X is the head, −Y is up, +Z is the model's left. INF marks an inference from the drawings.

## Build order

The booklet's order: torso (bags 1–2, steps 1–64), tail (bag 3, 65–94), leg 1 (bag 4, 95–133), leg 2 and the arms (bag 5, 134–186), head and jaw (bag 6, 187–236), then the nest (237–243).

## The rebuild against the inventory

`data/golden/31151-rex.ldr` has 603 parts; the T-rex needs 604. Per module: torso 198, head 67, jaw 35, tail 42 + 30 + 7 + 4, arms 12 + 12, thighs 65 + 65, feet 33 + 33.

The differences all follow what the booklet draws:
- dark green 85984 +3: step 39 clearly shows 6× in the torso, in one ridge along the spine. Steps 111/148 (legs) and 207–209 (head) add 2 + 2.
- dark green 29119 −2, 29120 −2: the booklet only shows 12 of the 16 (torso steps 43/50 and 54/61, head 197 and 211/213, legs 111/148). Likely the inventory and the drawings name some of these slopes differently.

The black 3021s are the head's rear tower and step 37's, under the step-40 cap between the shoulders.

Pins: 11 black 61332 are spread as torso 3 (steps 32–33), head 2 (eye pins) and legs 3 each. The legs' hip needs no extra pin: the 79505 disk clicks into the torso's 80563.

Leg geometry (steps 95–119): the 79180 ankle bracket lies across the slab. The flat slab is a zigzag: one 8×3 wedge plate from the flange to the knee (dark green 3001 and the 4×2 wedge bricks lie across it), then the second wedge from the grey 1×5 up to the hip cap beside the knee. Stood up, the leg is a Z with the knee at about 55 % of its height, and the hip is 282 LDU from the ankle.

Jaw width: re-checked on steps 229–232. Its core is 1 stud wide, with a tooth plate and a tan wedge plate on the side studs each side: 52 LDU (2.6 studs), narrower than the head (3.6). That is how the booklet builds it.

## Pose (P in the script)

body 40° nose-up, head −36° (carried high, about level), jaw 35° open, tail −46/−6/−8/−12 (rising from the hip), arms 100° and 115° about the shoulder ball (forward-down), thighs −2° and −11.3° against the torso (a short stride, both soles at the same height), feet flat. The stance width is fixed by the build: the hips are 1-DOF rotation joints.
